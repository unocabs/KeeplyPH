# Philippine motorcycle brand icons

54 logos for motorcycle, scooter and electric two-wheeler identification.

- `png/`: transparent 512px PNG exports with original artwork proportions.
- `webp/`: transparent 128px lossless WebP files used by Keeply.
- `preview.png`: visual index of the complete set.
- `sources.json`: original download URLs and preparation notes.

Some source images have lower native resolution; larger exports do not add detail. HATASU, Monarch and Husqvarna use visually checked imagegen-assisted cutouts. Logos remain trademarks of their owners; no open license or affiliation is asserted.
