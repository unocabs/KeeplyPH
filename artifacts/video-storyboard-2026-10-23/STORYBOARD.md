# Keeply introduction: Know what's next. Keep what's done.

Prepared 23 October 2026. Audience: people new to Keeply. Direction selected by the owner: problem to solution.

Create a calm, useful 35-second introduction. Begin with the effort of keeping household obligations in your head, show concrete records, and finish with one invitation. The screenshots were collected from the live website in Brave, using fictional sample records.

## Files and editing notes

- `raw/` contains five untouched Brave screenshot captures as JPEGs. The maintenance capture includes the full page so its item icon, name and service history remain available together.
- `annotated/` contains five AI-assisted PNG copies with captions and red highlights. These are editorial derivatives, not pixel-identical browser captures. The important visible amounts, dates, item names, identity artwork and captions were visually reviewed against the originals. Use the raw files as the authoritative source if rebuilding overlays in your editor.
- Use the numeric file order. Hold each image for the time above. Simple cuts or brief dissolves are enough. Optional slow movement can guide attention toward the highlighted area.
- Screenshots retain their native browser proportions; they have not been conformed to a video aspect ratio. Fit or reframe them in the final canvas. Keep each individual item's icon or provider logo alongside its readable name. Avoid shrinking the whole maintenance page until its text becomes unreadable; move between the heading and the highlighted history while retaining item identity.
- Keep estimate and missing-amount explanations visible whenever the payment total is shown. The total is based on saved upcoming expenses, includes estimates and excludes missing amounts. It is not an affordability calculation.
- The footage shows fictional records. Sample changes are previews, and sample alerts are not sent. The screenshots do not demonstrate notification delivery or provider-verified payments.
- Keep the added marketing copy branded as Keeply. Existing website branding remains visible in the original screenshots.
- This is an image pack and edit plan. No video or audio has been rendered, and no site code was changed for this work.

## Creative basis

The existing research index and baseline sections 2.1 to 2.3 and 12 informed this sequence: concrete household context, a clear next step, and retrievable records. This is an editorial proposal, not a tested claim about customer demand or Keeply's effectiveness. The sequence uses the existing positioning and features without changing the product or Free/Premium offering.
