# Utility and household biller logos

30 company identities, 35 service/category choices, one 128 × 128 transparent WebP source per company. Assets are local; the app does not depend on a third-party logo service. Combined size: 140552 bytes.

Shared identities: PLDT Home / PLDT landline, Globe At Home / Globe Postpaid, DITO Home / DITO Postpaid, SKY Fiber / SKY TruFiber / SKYcable, Cablelink Internet / cable TV. PLDT uses the corporate artwork to avoid showing Home branding on a telephone bill. SKY and Converge remain separate billers. BATELEC I and II remain separate cooperatives. Prepaid mobile brands are omitted from postpaid bills.

Rent and association dues accept custom landlord, property manager, homeowners' association or condominium corporation names and retain their existing house/building glyphs. All seven bill types accept custom billers. Provider names are explicit selections, never inferred from reminder titles. A missing image falls back to the bill-type glyph.

Most artwork comes from the company's website. Where those sites could not be fetched, the table records the actual alternate artwork source transparently: Wikimedia for PLDT, SKY, Globe, Smart, Manila Water and Boracay Water; payment provider ECPay for BATELEC II; Davao City Directory for the DCWD seal. The FOI Philippines icon was rejected during visual review because it is not the DCWD logo.
