# Lender logo artwork

30 provider/product choices share 28 source files in `public/lenders`. Every file is a transparent 128 × 128 WebP, padded to preserve the original mark's aspect ratio. Combined storage: 94,430 bytes. Bank logos are reused across loan categories; GLoan/GGives reuse `gcash.webp`, and SPayLater/SLoan reuse `shopee.webp`.

Marks identify the provider named on an existing agreement. The catalog is not a claim that each bank currently offers every selected loan type, and selecting a provider does not connect to its services. Motortrade is labeled as a dealer in the picker. Other dealers/financing companies can use Other lender with their own name.

Use original provider or corporate artwork rather than generated interpretations. Some official sites blocked asset retrieval; the corresponding published marks came from Wikimedia. The AEON mark is sourced from AEON Credit's corporate site in Malaysia and uses the shared AEON Credit wordmark, without a country-specific product claim.
