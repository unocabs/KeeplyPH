Most artwork comes from company assets. ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity use the pinned Simple Icons 15.19.0 vectors; GitHub Copilot uses GitHub’s official Octicons. Midjourney uses the Wikimedia reproduction of its sailboat mark. Leonardo.Ai uses Google’s cached 128 px favicon for leonardo.ai because the direct site rejected automated requests; the help-center wordmark was unsuitable at calendar size.

Artwork is centered with transparent padding and preserves interior white details. Claude retains its terracotta color. Unselected services, Other AI service, unknown IDs and failed images use the local sparkles category icon. Existing streaming and gym assets are unchanged.

Do not infer a service from a reminder’s personal name. Users explicitly choose a service; renaming a reminder preserves that selection.
